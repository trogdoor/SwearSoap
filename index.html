Clean-language filtering, made simple

Enjoy YouTube with fewer unwanted words.

SwearSoap creates a filter on your Windows PC, then automatically mutes selected language during playback. SwearSoap currently requires uninterrupted video playback. Videos interrupted by YouTube ads may not clean correctly. YouTube Premium or another ad-free viewing environment avoids this limitation.

How it works

One click prepares the video.

SwearSoap guides you through the complete process without requiring a terminal or technical setup.

1

Choose a video

Open a normal YouTube video and select the SwearSoap extension.

2

Let SwearSoap clean it

The video is temporarily muted while SwearSoap scans it and creates a local filter.

3

Press play

When you see “All clean!”, start the video. Selected language is muted automatically.

Designed for privacy

Your audio is processed on your computer.

SwearSoap does not require an account or upload captured audio to a SwearSoap cloud service. The Windows application and bundled language model do the work locally.

  • No SwearSoap account
  • No advertising or analytics
  • No saved transcripts
  • Captured audio is deleted after processing is attempted

Built for clarity

Always know what is happening.

Clear on-video messages show when SwearSoap is scanning, finishing the filter, and ready for you to watch.
